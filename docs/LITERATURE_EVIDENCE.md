# Literature Evidence & Scientific Validation

Every architectural and medical-engineering decision within AURA-MOM PRO must be grounded in verified academic literature, as per the Vishwakarma Awards evidence standard.

## 1. Non-Invasive Fetal ECG Extraction
**Claim:** Fetal electrocardiography can be successfully extracted from maternal abdominal signals using advanced decomposition or deep learning techniques.
*   *Towards reconstruction of Pulsed-wave Doppler signals from Non-invasive fetal ECG* (Verma et al., 2025). [arXiv:2501.16651]
*   *Clifford wavelets for fetal ECG extraction* (Jallouli et al., 2021). [arXiv:2103.04085]
*   *Integrating Contrastive Learning and Cycle Generative Adversarial Networks for Non-invasive Fetal ECG Extraction.* (Qu et al., 2025). [doi:10.1007/s00246-024-03633-3]

## 2. Maternal ECG Cancellation (Classical DSP / NLMS)
**Claim:** Maternal QRS complexes can be adaptively cancelled from abdominal mixtures before fetal QRS detection.
*   *Efficient fetal-maternal ECG signal separation from two channel maternal abdominal ECG via diffusion-based channel selection* (Li et al., 2017). [arXiv:1702.02025]
*   *Advanced Bioelectrical Signal Processing Methods: Past, Present and Future Approach-Part I: Cardiac Signals.* (Martinek et al., 2021). [doi:10.3390/s21155186]

## 3. Multimodal Analysis (Acoustic & Electrical)
**Claim:** Combining phonocardiography (acoustic) and electrical signals enhances robustness.
*   *Acquisition Devices for Fetal Phonocardiography: A Scoping Review.* (Giordano et al., 2024). [doi:10.3390/bioengineering11040367]
